02
Text Representation & Encoding

Models need numbers, not words — turning cleaned text into vectors that capture frequency and importance.

One-hot encoding
"cat"→[0,0,1,0,0]
Bag of words
the cat sat the mat
→{the: 2, cat: 1, sat: 1, mat: 1}
N-grams
the cat sat
the catcat sat
TF-IDF
"rare"→weight 0.82
"the"→weight 0.02
TF-IDF(t,d) = tf(t,d) × log(N / df(t))
03
Word Embeddings

Instead of counting words, embeddings place them in a continuous space where meaning and context shape the geometry.

Embedding space (vector arithmetic)
king − man + woman ≈ queen
Word2Vec — CBOW
the ___ sat→cat
Word2Vec — Skip-gram
cat→the, sat
Cosine similarity
cos(king, queen)=0.82
cos(king, banana)=0.05
GloVe
co-occurs(ice, cold)→high
cos(θ) = (A · B) / (‖A‖ ‖B‖) ← word similarity

Language Modeling & Sequence Tasks

Predicting what comes next — the backbone of autocomplete, translation, and text generation.

N-gram language model
"the cat ___"→sat (highest P)
Sequence-to-sequence
"I am fine" (EN)
→"मैं ठीक हूँ" (HI)
Text generation
"Once upon a"→"time..."
Perplexity
model A: 12.4
model B: 6.1 (better)
Perplexity = 2^(−1/N Σ log₂ P(wᵢ))
05
Information Extraction

Pulling structured facts — people, places, relationships — out of unstructured sentences.

Named entity recognition

Chunking

→[NP the cat] [VP sat down]
Dependency parsing
The cat sat
→subj(cat, sat)
Coreference resolution

→she = Maria
B-PER I-PER O O B-ORG O ← BIO tagging scheme

Attention & Transformer-based NLP

Letting a model weigh every word against every other word at once — the shift that produced modern language models.

Attention mechanism

BERT & transfer learning

→fine-tuned for sentiment
Multi-head attention
head 1: syntax
head 2: coreference
