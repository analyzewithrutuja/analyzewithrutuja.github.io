Image Fundamentals & Preprocessing

Every vision model starts with pixels: getting color, contrast, and scale right before a network ever sees the image. The convolution and stride math below is what later determines a detector's receptive field (how much of the image each prediction actually "sees"), so getting it right here shapes everything downstream in a detection or segmentation pipeline.

Image Classification & Transfer Learning

Deciding what an image shows, and standing on the shoulders of models already trained on millions of images. In vision work, transfer learning usually means taking an ImageNet-pretrained backbone (ResNet, EfficientNet) and swapping just the head: freeze the early layers that already recognize edges and textures, then fine-tune the later ones for your specific classes or detection task.

Advanced Vision Models

Beyond the standard CNN: attention, self-supervision, and generative models reshaping what vision systems can do. GANs and diffusion models earn their keep here by generating and augmenting image data itself (synthesizing extra training examples for a rare class, or upsampling weak frames) rather than the general-purpose synthesis those same architectures do elsewhere.
